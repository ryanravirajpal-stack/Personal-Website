Please note that as the name implies, Taco Bell will be catered. The following questions are concerned with catering food and beverages.

Regarding Alcohol

IF YOU ARE DRINKING, please note that there is NOT an open bar, as that would be far too expensive. As such, each person will be provided with THREE (3) drink tickets, not including ONE (1) separate shot voucher and the ONE (1) spiked Baja Blast Freeze – if you indicated for one.

This is to ensure that everyone has a good time within healthy and responsible limits. There are also a number of people who will not use all their tickets, or any of them; if you would like to use more than your allotted number of drink tickets, please feel free to socialize and ask around to see if anyone has any extra tickets they are not using. Regardless, everyone will be capped at FIVE (5) cocktails and TWO (2) shots. As everyone is an adult, I trust that you will know your limits and drink (or whatever else) responsibly.

Note: Final call at the bar will be held between 9:30 and 9:40. A selection of drinks from the "Bartending" page will be available during the party. While not yet finalized, you can familiarize yourself with potential drinks by visiting the Bartending page.

Regarding the Pool

Due to request, the pool will be open for use during the party. However, we ask that all guests adhere to the following guidelines:

  • No beverages of any kind are allowed in the pool.
  • Please no excessive splashing or roughhousing.

Thank you for your cooperation! Please note that no shower will be available for use during the party. However, if you wish to rinse off the chlorine after using the pool, there will be a hose available for use by the pool entry ladder.
